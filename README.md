AHMU-01 — Avionics Health Monitoring Unit

Project Status: Conceptual Design / Simulation-Based Development / Not Physically Tested

1. Project Overview

The AHMU-01 is a conceptual avionics health monitoring unit developed as an educational hardware engineering project.

The project explores the design of an electronic monitoring system intended to acquire environmental data, identify predefined abnormal conditions, and provide diagnostic information to support equipment maintenance.

The development approach emphasizes electronic circuit design, PCB development, simulation, systems engineering, and verification planning, incorporating engineering practices relevant to the aerospace industry.

2. Mission and Purpose

2.1 Mission Statement

The mission of the AHMU-01 is to monitor environmental and operational conditions within an aircraft's avionics bay, providing diagnostic information to support the identification of abnormal conditions and maintenance activities.

2.2 Monitoring Purpose

The AHMU-01 is intended to provide information about equipment temperature and selected operating conditions, helping maintenance personnel identify potential anomalies and assess equipment health indicators.

2.3 Operational Context

Environmental monitoring can provide useful information about the operating conditions of electronic equipment installed in aircraft avionics compartments.

Within this context, the AHMU-01 is conceived as an auxiliary monitoring device that contributes to equipment condition assessment without replacing existing aircraft monitoring or safety-critical systems.

2.4 Communication Purpose

The AHMU-01 is intended to make monitoring and diagnostic information available to an external receiving system, supporting equipment status reporting and fault investigation.

2.5 Diagnostic Purpose

The AHMU-01 aims to support the early identification of predefined abnormal equipment conditions, providing information that may assist troubleshooting and maintenance activities.

3. Project Scope and Limitations

This project is intended for engineering portfolio purposes.

The AHMU-01 is not a certified avionics device and is not intended for installation or operation on an aircraft.

The project's initial scope focuses on electronic hardware design, simulation, PCB development, and the preparation of engineering documentation.

Physical hardware validation, environmental qualification, and formal certification are outside the scope of the initial development phase.
